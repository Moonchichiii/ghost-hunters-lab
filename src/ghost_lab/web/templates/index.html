Ghost in the Machine / an open-weight experiment

We opened an AI model to see if we could change one thought without breaking everything else.

We found a signal linked to ghosts inside Gemma. Now we're testing what happens when we turn it up, and what else changes.

In 1949 Gilbert Ryle challenged the idea of a "ghost in the machine" in The Concept of Mind. We borrow the metaphor, not the question: this is not a test of whether AI has a mind.
